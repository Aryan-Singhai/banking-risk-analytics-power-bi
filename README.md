# Banking & Risk Analytics | Power BI

> 🚧 **Project Status: In Progress**

A Power BI business intelligence project focused on banking performance, customer behaviour, risk analysis, segmentation, and decision-oriented reporting.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to transform banking data into an interactive analytical report designed to help identify important performance and risk patterns and support data-driven business decisions.

The project focuses on the complete Power BI workflow:

- Data preparation and transformation using Power Query
- Data modelling and relationship design
- DAX measures and calculated metrics
- KPI development
- Risk and customer segmentation
- Interactive dashboard design
- Business insights and recommendations

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to analyse banking data from multiple business perspectives, identify meaningful patterns in customer and risk behaviour, and present the findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The final analysis will answer key business questions around performance, customer segments, and risk exposure.
